# Terms and Conditions

This competition is a research / training benchmark built on top of
[Co-Study4Grid](https://github.com/marota/Expert_op4grid_recommender) and the
[Codabench](https://www.codabench.org/) platform.

- Submissions are recorded Co-Study4Grid Game Mode sessions (`game_session.json`).
- You may submit sessions you produced yourself, by hand or with an automated
  agent driving the Co-Study4Grid workspace.
- Submissions are scored automatically against the official study manifest. The
  organizers reserve the right to invalidate submissions whose recorded physical
  results are inconsistent with the network model.
